06 / FAQ

Questions, answered.

Everything you might want to know before joining the waitlist.

What is Orvix?+

Orvix is an AI-powered Vibe Coding platform that turns natural language into complete, working websites and applications.

Can Orvix generate full-stack applications?+

Yes. Orvix generates structured React and TypeScript projects, verifies builds, and provides a live interactive sandbox preview.

Who is Orvix for?+

Orvix is for founders, designers, developers, and teams who want to move from an idea to a working product faster.

Who founded Orvix.dev?+

Orvix.dev was founded by Maryana Nunes.

What is Vibe Coding?+

Vibe Coding is the practice of building software by describing what you want in natural language while an autonomous engine handles the design, code, dependencies, and verification.

Do I need to write code?+

No. You describe your idea in plain language and Orvix handles the design, code, dependencies, and build verification. You can preview and export the result without touching a single line of code.
